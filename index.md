Welcome to my website! I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biology at St. Francis College. 

## Research interests
I studied cytoskeletal biology using high-resolution microscopic imaging in the past, and before working at St. Francis College, I applied my knowledge and experience to elucidate molecular mechanisms of anti-cancer drug resistance. I characterize myself as a Cell and Cancer Biologist built on a solid foundation of Biochemistry, Molecular Biology, and Microbiology. At St. Francis College, I began doable research with undergraduate students. I have basically two projects; i) one focusing on finding intesting mutations in cancer and bring it to the bench, and ii) creating new biomaterials (cryogels). My first few years of outcomes with students are briefly highlighted in this page: [Celebrating firsts with professor Kita and his students](https://www.sfc.edu/stories/celebrating-firsts-with-professor-kita-and-his-students). I have actively developed collaborative relationships with R1 institutions. Since 2025, I have also taken a part of Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI) Science Education Alliance-Phage Hunters Advancing Genomics and Evolutionary Science (SEA-PHAGES) to implement authentic research for all Biology major students.

## Teaching
I have taught Introduction to Cell Biology (and lab), Genetics (and lab), and Molecular Biology (and lab). I also sometimes help teach Microbiology (and lab). As I briefly described in my research interests, I have progressively incorporate C.U.R.E. (Course-based Undergraduate Research Experiences) in my lab courses. In addition to HHMI SEA-PHAGES, BASIL (Biochemistry Authentic Scientific Inquiry Laboratory; led by Rochester Institute of Technology) and D2D (Design to Data; led by the University of California, Davis) will soon be incorporated in my teaching, making St. Francis College as the only NYC 4-year college/university offering nationwide consortia-supported C.U.R.E.
